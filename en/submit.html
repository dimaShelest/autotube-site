Subscriber stories

Did you live something worth telling? Send us your story, unsigned. If we pick it, we adapt it and tell it in a 3D-animated video (in Spanish), with names, places and dates changed.

Coming soon

The story form opens very soon. Meanwhile you can read how we will handle your story in the privacy policy and the terms.

What we are looking for

  • Something you lived (or that the person who lived it told you directly and you may share).
  • With a twist, a hard decision, a tense moment or an ending nobody expected.
  • That can be pictured: places, objects, a journey, one particular night.

What we do not publish

  • Accusations against recognisable people, companies or institutions.
  • Illegal content, graphic violence, self-harm, or stories of minors in situations of risk.
  • Made-up stories presented as true, or stories copied from elsewhere.

There is no payment, prize or reward for sending a story, and we do not promise to publish it. The pseudonym mention is optional.

If you are going through a hard time or thinking about hurting yourself, please do not carry it alone: in Spain call 024 (free, 24 h); elsewhere, call your local helpline or the emergency number (112 / 911). This form is not a support service and is not read in real time.

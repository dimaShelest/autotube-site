Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · consent version 2026-10-27

This policy covers three things: (A) the stories subscribers send us through the form, (B) the autotube application and YouTube API data, and (C) this website. In short: we ask for the minimum, anonymise it before use, delete it early, and never sell or share it. The Spanish version is the reference text.

1. Controller

Data controller: [controller's name — pending], owner of the Voz sin Firma channel (https://www.youtube.com/@your-channel). Contact for any privacy matter: contact@example.com.

A. Subscriber stories

If your story contains special-category data about yourself (for example about your health), by sending it you explicitly consent to its processing for this purpose (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR); you can leave such details out or keep them general.

A2. Purpose and legal basis

  • Assessing, adapting and publishing your story in anonymised form in the channel's videos: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the story with your code or by writing to us; withdrawal does not affect what was done before.
  • Preventing spam and abuse (Turnstile, limits per IP hash, honeypot field): our legitimate interest in keeping the form usable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), with minimal data and quick deletion.

We make no automated decisions with legal effects on you: automatic rules help sort submissions and detect problematic content, but a person decides which stories are used.

A3. Moderation

We turn down stories that look invented or copied, that accuse recognisable people or organisations, or that contain illegal content, graphic violence or self-harm, and stories that expose minors in situations of risk. Stories sent by people under 18 are deleted.

A4. Data minimisation and anonymisation

  • As soon as your story has been downloaded from the server to the owner's computer, the text, pseudonym, e-mail and IP hash are erased from the server; only the identifier, the dates and the code hash remain there (so the code keeps working).
  • Before any use we replace first names, surnames, places, dates, addresses, phone numbers, e-mails, links, licence plates and ID numbers with consistent fictional equivalents. The mapping table is private, kept only on the owner's computer, and is never published or shared.
  • The video tells an adaptation: it may summarise, dramatise and change details. On screen it says «Historia enviada por un suscriptor, nombres cambiados» (story sent by a subscriber, names changed).
  • If we ever use an artificial-intelligence service to help moderate or adapt stories, it will receive only the already anonymised text, never your e-mail or pseudonym, and we will add it to the list of processors on this page before using it.

A6. Who processes the data (processors) and transfers

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — runs the form server (Workers), stores submissions in its D1 database (preferred location: Western Europe) and provides the Turnstile spam check. It acts as processor under its data processing addendum; any transfers to the US rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Page hosting (GitHub Pages and/or Cloudflare): serve these pages and may log technical access data (such as the IP address) under their own policies.
  • The channel owner's computer, where stories are moderated and adapted.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, do not use it for advertising or profiling, and do not train AI models with it.

A7. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and you may withdraw your consent at any time:

  • Immediate erasure: at Delete a story with your code.
  • Other rights: write to contact@example.com quoting your deletion code. As we never ask for identity data, the code is how we check the story is yours (Art. 11 GDPR). We reply within one month.
  • If the story has already been published, we delete the original and the names table; the video is anonymised, but if you ask us to take it down we will consider it in good faith.
  • You can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or the supervisory authority of your country.

A8. Minimum age

The form is for people aged 18 or over only. If we learn that a story was sent by a minor, we delete it.

A9. Security

HTTPS connections; the deletion code and the IP are stored only as cryptographic hashes; the download endpoint is protected by a secret token held only by the owner's program; the server does not log request contents; the working copy and the names table are kept outside any public repository.

B. The autotube application and YouTube data

autotube is the desktop tool the owner uses to upload Voz sin Firma's videos to their own channel. Only the owner uses it, with their own Google account. Contact: contact@example.com.

B1. Data it accesses

autotube uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) with Google OAuth 2.0, client type "Desktop app". The owner authorizes access once on Google's consent screen; the response is received by a temporary local server on 127.0.0.1 on their own computer. Only these three scopes are requested:

ScopeOperations autotube performs
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.uploadvideos.insert (upload the owner's videos), thumbnails.set (custom thumbnail). channelBanners.insert and watermarks.set (below) accept this scope or youtube.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtubeplaylists.list with mine=true (find the series playlist by title among the channel's own playlists), playlists.insert (create it if missing), playlistItems.insert (add the uploaded videos); videos.list and videos.delete only for the program's OWN private test uploads (read back the metadata it just uploaded, then remove that test video).
Setting up the owner's own channel (autotube channel setup / check): channels.list with mine=true (read the authorised channel: ID, title, public @handle, description, keywords, country, language, banner and profile-picture URLs, and status: privacy, linked, long-upload eligibility, made-for-kids flag); channels.update with part=brandingSettings (write the description, keywords, country, language, banner and, if configured, the channel trailer); channelBanners.insert (upload the channel banner image); watermarks.set (upload the watermark YouTube shows on the channel's videos); playlists.insert also creates, once, the channel's public playlist for each format.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-sslRead-only access to the comments on the channel's OWN videos, to collect topic suggestions (autotube ideas collect, at most once a day): channels.list with mine=true (the channel's uploads list), playlistItems.list (IDs of its own videos), videos.list with part=statistics (comment count, so an unchanged video is not read again) and commentThreads.list (published comments and comments held for review, with the replies the API returns). commentThreads.list accepts only this scope. autotube never writes, replies to, moderates or deletes comments.

Although the youtube scope is broad, autotube only uses it for the operations listed. The only data it reads from YouTube are the IDs and titles of the channel's own playlists, the metadata of its own uploads (video ID, title, description, tags, privacy status) and the own channel's public settings and status listed above, to check they match what was intended. API quota cost: reads 1 unit; channels.update, channelBanners.insert, watermarks.set, playlists.insert, playlistItems.insert, thumbnails.set and videos.delete 50 units each; videos.insert 1600.

Of viewers' data, autotube only reads the comments they leave on the channel's own videos (youtube.force-ssl scope, above) and keeps the minimum listed in B2. autotube does not access audience analytics, subscribers, other channels or other accounts, and does not request the Google e-mail address or profile. Subscriber stories (part A) arrive through this website's form, not through the YouTube API.

B3. How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's videos to their own channel, set their thumbnails, organise them in the channel's playlists, set up the presentation of the owner's own channel (description, keywords, country, language, banner, watermark) and check it is ready to publish, and to collect from the comments on its own videos the topics the audience asks for (legal basis: legitimate interest, GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). If a video is made from one of those ideas, the person who suggested it first is thanked on screen and in the description by their public name ("Idea de @user"); no prizes or payments are ever offered for comments. It is not used for advertising, profiling, selling data or training artificial-intelligence models.

B4. Sharing with third parties

The data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. autotube talks to the YouTube API only through Google's servers (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com). The refresh token is sent only to Google's token endpoint. autotube contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

B5. Retention and deletion

  • The token is kept until the owner deletes it or revokes access.
  • To remove access completely: delete secrets/youtube_token.json and revoke autotube's permission at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google account permissions page). After revocation the token stops working.
  • Upload results, the channel state and the quota log are local records the owner can delete at any time by removing the files listed in B2.
  • Videos and playlists remain on YouTube under the owner's control, managed in YouTube Studio.
  • Ideas from comments: every video is read again at least every 7 days and anything not refreshed for 30 days is deleted; a comment deleted on YouTube disappears at the next read; a rejected idea loses its quotes and names; the name of the person who suggested a produced idea is kept while the thank-you is shown. Anyone can ask for their name or quote to be removed by writing to contact@example.com.

B6. Security

All communication with Google uses HTTPS. The OAuth flow uses a random state parameter and a local redirect to 127.0.0.1. The token file has mode 600 and is never published or pushed to a repository.

B7. Google and YouTube services

autotube uses the YouTube API Services. By using autotube, the YouTube Terms of Service apply, as does the Google Privacy Policy.

autotube's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

C. This website

C1. No tracking cookies

The site is static: no first-party cookies, analytics, advertising or trackers. Only the Send your story and Delete a story pages run a small first-party script, which only sends what you type to the form server.

C2. Cloudflare Turnstile

To stop spam, the form page loads Cloudflare Turnstile when you start typing. Turnstile processes technical data about your browser and connection (such as the IP) to tell people from bots, without showing ads or tracking you across sites, and may use storage strictly necessary for that check. It is the only third-party component of the site. More: Turnstile privacy policy.

C3. Hosting

The hosting provider (GitHub Pages and/or Cloudflare) may log technical access data (for example the IP address) under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page, its date and the consent version will be updated; material changes will not apply to stories already sent without your consent. Questions: contact@example.com.

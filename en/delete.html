Delete a story

Enter the code you received when you sent your story. We delete it from our server and, at the next sync, also the working copy and the private table of changed names.

Coming soon

Story submissions are not open yet, so there is nothing to delete. Any questions: contact@example.com.

What if it was already published?

If your story already appears in a video, deleting it removes the original text, your pseudonym, your e-mail and the private table of changed names. The video is an anonymised adaptation; if you still want it taken down, write to contact@example.com with your code and we will consider it. Details in the privacy policy.

Lost your code? We cannot recover it (we only keep a cryptographic hash). Write to us with details of the story and the approximate date you sent it and we will do our best to find it.

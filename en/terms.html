2. Sending stories

2.1 Who may send and what

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • The story must be yours (you lived it) or you must have the right to tell it, and it must be true to the best of your knowledge. Do not send texts copied from elsewhere or fiction presented as fact.

You remain the owner of your story and may tell it anywhere you like.

  • There is no payment, prize, draw or reward of any kind for sending a story or for its publication.
  • We are not obliged to use your story or to explain why we did not. We may turn it down without notice.
  • If we use it, the video will say «Historia enviada por un suscriptor, nombres cambiados» (story sent by a subscriber, names changed) and, only if you gave us one, mention your pseudonym. We may leave out a pseudonym that is inappropriate or matches a real name in the story.

2.4 Withdrawal

You can delete your story at any time with your code at Delete a story. Before publication, the story is no longer used. If it is already published, we delete the original text and your data; the video is an anonymised adaptation that stays online, but we will consider in good faith any reasonable take-down request sent to contact@example.com with your code.
